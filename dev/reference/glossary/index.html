    Glossary¶

    These are the names MDAPY uses. Every module follows them, so a concept has one name everywhere.

    Data model¶

    Name Meaning
    System One atomic configuration: a Box, a table of per-atom properties and global information. Immutable: operations return a new System.
    Box The simulation box: three lattice vectors, an origin and periodicity flags.
    box.matrix (3, 3) array whose rows are the lattice vectors a, b, c.
    box.pbc (3,) bool array, True where the box is periodic.
    Trajectory A sequence of frames, read lazily.
    frame One System of a Trajectory.
    table The per-atom table of a System, a polars DataFrame with one row per atom.
    system["name"] The values of a column, a component or an expression over the columns, one per atom, as a read-only NumPy array.
    condition A Python expression over the columns of a system that System.filter keeps atoms by, such as "positions.z > 10 and element == 'Cu'". See Selecting and ordering atoms.
    component One value of an array column, written column.component: x, y, z for 3 values, x, y, z, w for 4, xx … zz by rows for 9, and the index from 0 for any number, such as positions.z or group.1.
    info Global information of a System, such as energy or timestep.
    NeighborList Pairs of atoms within a cutoff, the nearest neighbors of each atom, or the atoms that share a Voronoi face, including periodic images.
    pair One entry of a NeighborList: atoms i and j and the periodic image shifts of j.
    shifts Periodic image of the second atom of a pair, (3,) int8: the pair vector is x[j] - x[i] + shifts @ box.matrix.
    index_dtype Integer type of atom indices in results such as NeighborList.j: int32, or int64 in a build with 64-bit indices.
    TriangleMesh A mesh of triangles, which may cross the periodic boundaries of a box: vertices, faces and the periodic image shifts of each corner.
    Topology Bonds, angles, dihedrals and impropers of a system; each a table with atoms (0-based row indices), type and optional type_label.
    format Name of a file format, passed as format=, such as extxyz. See Reading and writing files.
    Calculator Interface that computes energy, forces and virial for a System.
    interop The module that converts to and from the objects of other packages: to_ase, from_ase, to_ase_calculator, from_ase_calculator, to_ovito, from_ovito, to_pymatgen, from_pymatgen, to_freud, from_freud.
    average atom The single species of an average-atom potential, standing for a random alloy; A in potential files.
    Result Base class of the objects returned by analyses.
    reference A reference configuration, for example in atomic strain analysis.
    unwrapped positions Positions moved by whole periodic images so that no cluster or molecule is cut by the periodic boundaries, or, along a trajectory, so that atoms move continuously through them; with an image column, the positions plus the image flags times the lattice vectors.
    affine transformation A map of positions x to M x + t, with a (3, 3) matrix M and a translation t: a rotation, shear, scaling or move, or a mix of them; vectors such as velocities turn with M alone.
    selection Some atoms of a system, given as a boolean mask of one value per atom or as their indices.
    mask (N,) bool, true for the atoms of a selection; System.where gives the mask of conditions, and analyses take one as atoms.
    overlapping atoms Atoms closer than a distance to another, such as the copies of an atom or the atoms where structures put together clash; find_overlapping selects them.
    spline A curve through values, cubic between neighboring points, whose first and second derivatives are continuous; Spline along a line and GridSpline on a grid.
    least squares A fit that makes the sum of the squared residuals, the values less the curve, as small as it can, each weighed by 1/σ² when the values have standard deviations σ; fit_polynomial, fit_surface, fit_curve.
    equation of state The energy of a crystal as a function of its volume, of four parameters: the energy and the volume at the minimum, the bulk modulus there and its derivative with respect to the pressure; fit_eos.
    orthogonal cell A box of the same crystal whose lattice vectors lie along x, y and z: along each, the shortest combination of the lattice vectors of the box that lies along it; orthogonal_cell makes one.
    copy One of the systems replicate places side by side; copy (i, j, l) of (na, nb, nc) is number i + na(j + nb l).
    lag The number of frames between two frames of a trajectory.
    time average The mean of a quantity over the frames of a trajectory, of each atom for a column of the atoms.
    time series A quantity in each frame of a trajectory, such as a column reduced to one number.
    reduction One number from the values of a column of the atoms, such as their mean or sum; reduce and its OPERATIONS.
    histogram The counts of the values of a column in bins of equal width.
    frame difference The change of a column of each atom from a reference frame, the atoms matched by id; the column <name>_change.
    frozen value The value a column has in a reference frame, given to the atoms of another by freeze; the column <name>_frozen.
    smoothed trajectory The frames of a trajectory with their positions and other real numbers averaged over a window of frames about each.
    trajectory line The path of an atom over the frames of a trajectory, followed through the periodic boundaries.
    combined system The atoms of two systems in one, in the box of the first, as combine makes it; OVITO calls it combining datasets.
    drift The motion of the center of mass of all the atoms, or of some, over a trajectory; remove_drift moves each frame to keep the center of mass where it is in a reference frame.
    file pattern A file name with *, standing for the files it matches, in the order of the numbers in their names, such as dump.*.lammpstrj.
    topology of a trajectory A system, usually read from a LAMMPS data file, whose bonds, charges, molecules and other columns the frames of a trajectory take, their atoms matched by id.
    spanning cluster A cluster that joins a periodic image of itself through its bonds, so that it has no unbroken position.
    ring A closed path of bonds through distinct atoms; a shortest-path ring has no two atoms closer through the network than along it.
    covalent bond A pair of atoms closer than the sum of their covalent radii and 0.45 Å, as pictures draw them.
    bond length distribution The counts of the lengths of the bonds of a topology, each measured to the nearest periodic image, in total and for each pair of elements.
    isosurface The surface where a field over the box, such as a binned property of the atoms, equals a level; it bounds the regions where the field is above it.
    voxel A cell of a grid that divides the box along its vectors, with the value of a field.
    coordination polyhedron The convex hull of the neighbors of an atom, such as the octahedron of oxygen about the titanium of a perovskite.
    vector arrow An arrow drawn from an atom along a vector of it, such as its displacement, velocity or force: a shaft and a head 2.5 times as wide and 4.5 times as long as the radius of the shaft.
    color column The color column of a system, (N, 3) sRGB from 0 to 1, that color_coding, color_by_type and assign_color set and render draws by default; a radius column likewise sets the radii.
    colormap A map of values to colors, one of COLORMAPS: rainbow, viridis, magma, jet, hot, blue_white_red and gray as OVITO computes them, plasma and coolwarm, and labels for discrete values.
    file of a frame Trajectory.file_of(i), the path of the file that holds frame i of a trajectory, without reading the frame.
    molecular polycrystal A polycrystal of whole molecules, from a unit with a molecule column: build_polycrystal.
    gradient polycrystal A polycrystal whose grain size changes along one direction, from the seeds of gradient_seeds.
    pressure-temperature diagram The stable phase of one element at each pressure and temperature, from the free energies of its phases: pressure_temperature_diagram, its boundaries with their slopes by the Clausius-Clapeyron relation.
    label colors LABEL_COLORS, twelve distinct colors given in turn to the values of a column colored by type or through the discrete colormap labels.
    type colors TYPE_COLORS, the nine colors of OVITO in turn for atom types without elements, named Type 1, Type 2 and so on by atom_names when drawn.
    global illumination Light that reaches a surface after bouncing off others, as the shiny style of pictures traces it.
    showing a picture show_image puts a picture from render, or a file, on the page of a notebook.
    style of a picture Its lights, shadows, occlusion and outlines together: fast (as the viewports of OVITO), matte (as its Tachyon renderer), shiny (after its OSPRay renderer) or flat (outlined, as ASE draws atoms).
    highlight The light of a lamp mirrored by a surface toward the viewer: a bright spot on a sphere.
    metalness From 0 to 1, how much a surface behaves as a metal: it reflects less light diffusely and its highlights take its color.
    depth of field In a picture through a lens, the range of depths that looks sharp around the plane in focus; nearer and farther things blur.
    soft shadow A shadow cast by a light of some size: dark where all of the light is hidden, fading where part of it is.
    ionic step One configuration of a VASP relaxation or molecular dynamics, with its energy, forces and virial; one frame of an OUTCAR file.
    training set Frames with reference energies, forces and virials, usually from DFT, in extended XYZ, to fit a machine-learned potential.
    SOAP The smooth overlap of atomic positions: the power spectrum of the Gaussian density of the neighbors of an atom, a descriptor of its neighborhood that does not change when it is turned, moved or reordered.
    structure descriptor The mean of the descriptors of the atoms of a frame, one point per frame.
    farthest point sampling Choosing points one by one, each the farthest from those chosen before.
    label A reference energy, force or virial of a frame, usually from DFT, that a model is trained to predict.
    parity plot The predictions of a model against the labels, one point each; on the diagonal where they agree.
    committee Several models of one system, such as models trained with other seeds; the spread of their predictions marks where they were not trained enough.
    model deviation The largest over the atoms of a frame of the spread of the forces of a committee, as DP-GEN chooses frames by it.
    novelty The distance of a frame from the nearest frame of a training set in the space of descriptors.
    net force The sum of the forces on all atoms of a frame: zero for a crystal or a cluster in vacuum.
    crystal cluster Neighboring atoms of one structure whose neighbors map onto each other by rotations of the lattice: one region with one lattice frame, as the dislocation analysis joins them.
    ideal vector The vector between two atoms in the perfect, unstrained crystal, in the lattice frame of a crystal cluster.
    spatial correlation function The mean of one property of the atoms at a place times another at a distance r from it, ⟨P₁(0) P₂(r)⟩; of two properties equal to 1, the radial distribution function.
    elastic strain The strain of the lattice about an atom of a crystal against the ideal crystal of given lattice constants, from the deformation gradient that maps the ideal vectors onto its neighbors; it leaves out the slip of dislocations.
    good tetrahedron A tetrahedron of the Delaunay tessellation whose six edges have ideal vectors that add up to zero around each face: crystal there maps onto the perfect lattice.
    interface mesh The triangles between the good tetrahedra and the rest, on which Burgers circuits run.
    Burgers circuit A closed path along the edges of the interface mesh; the sum of the ideal vectors of its edges is the Burgers vector of the dislocations it encloses.
    defect mesh The surface around the defects that are not dislocations: the interface mesh without the triangles the lines took, closed over their free ends.
    dislocation core, core atoms The bad crystal the Burgers circuits of a dislocation line enclose: the tetrahedra that are not good and not empty space behind the triangles of the interface mesh the circuits took. Its core atoms are the atoms at their corners, of any structure.
    family of Burgers vectors The Burgers vectors of one kind, the same in any order and sign of their components, such as 1/2<110> or 1/6<112>.
    CA file OVITO's crystal analysis file: the box, the structures and their families of Burgers vectors, the crystal clusters, the dislocation lines with their junctions and the defect mesh, as DislocationResult.write writes it and read_dislocations reads it.

    Per-atom properties¶

    Column names are lowercase snake_case. Vector quantities are stored in one column of fixed-size arrays of length 3 and use the plural name.

    Column Type Unit Meaning
    positions (3,) float64 Å positions
    velocities (3,) float64 Å/ps velocities
    forces (3,) float64 eV/Å forces
    element element chemical symbol
    type int32 LAMMPS atom type, starting at 1
    id int64 atom ID, starting at 1
    molecule int64 molecule ID (LAMMPS molecule-ID); from ChemicalSpecies, numbered from 1 by decreasing size
    formula str chemical formula of a molecule in Hill order, such as H2O, CO2, H3N
    type_label str name of the atom type, for example a force-field type
    mass float64 amu mass
    charge float64 e charge
    image (3,) int32 periodic image flags
    atom_name str name of the atom in its residue, as in PDB files, such as CA
    residue, residue_number str, int64 name and number of the residue of an atom, as in PDB files
    movable (3,) bool whether the atom may move along x, y, z (VASP selective dynamics)
    cna_structure, ptm_structure, ackland_jones_structure, diamond_structure, chill_plus_structure int8 structure type from an identification, named after the method; the result names the types
    grain int64 grain of an atom from GrainSegmentation, numbered from 0 by decreasing size; -1 for none
    planar_fault int8 planar fault type of fcc crystals from PlanarFaults: non-hcp, other hcp, intrinsic stacking fault, coherent twin boundary, multilayer stacking fault, extrinsic stacking fault
    csp float64 Å² centro-symmetry parameter
    cnp float64 Å² common neighbor parameter
    coordination int64 number of neighbors; coordination_<element> counts those of one element
    q4, q6, … float64 Steinhardt order parameters, with the options in the name: q4_average, q4_voronoi, q4_average_voronoi, q4_voronoi_weighted
    solid, solid_bonds bool, int32 whether an atom is solid-like, and its number of solid-like bonds (Steinhardt solid=True)
    cluster int64 cluster of bonded atoms, numbered by decreasing size
    surface bool whether an atom lies on the surface of the solid (SurfaceMesh, surface=True)
    dxa_structure, dxa_crystal_cluster int8, int64 structure of an atom and its crystal cluster, −1 for none, in the dislocation analysis (DislocationAnalysis)
    dxa_core_atoms int64 the dislocation line whose core holds an atom, an index into the lines, −1 for none, with core_atoms=True (DislocationAnalysis)
    voronoi_volume, voronoi_coordination, cavity_radius float64, int64, float64 Å³, , Å volume and number of faces shared with atoms of the Voronoi cell, and the distance to its farthest vertex
    entropy float64 kB pair entropy fingerprint, with the options in the name: entropy_average, entropy_local_density, entropy_average_local_density
    temperature float64 K temperature of an atom and its neighbors
    lindemann float64 Lindemann index of an atom over a trajectory
    displacement, displacement_magnitude (3,) float64, float64 Å, Å displacement of an atom from a reference, and its length (AtomicDisplacement)
    site, occupancy index_dtype, int64 the nearest reference site of an atom, and the number of atoms on it
    ptm_rmsd, ptm_interatomic_distance float64 , Å deviation from the best template of polyhedral template matching, and the distance between nearest neighbors of its lattice
    ptm_orientation 4 float64 rotation of the local lattice from its standard orientation, a quaternion (x, y, z, w)
    ptm_deformation_gradient 9 float64 elastic deformation gradient of the local lattice, by rows
    ptm_ordering int8 chemical ordering of the local lattice from polyhedral template matching
    shear_strain, volumetric_strain, d2min float64 , , Å² von Mises and volumetric atomic strain against a reference, and the non-affine squared displacement
    elastic_strain, elastic_volumetric_strain float64, (3, 3) and scalar elastic strain of an atom of a crystal against the ideal crystal, in the spatial frame, and a third of its trace

    Energies and virials¶

    Name Meaning
    energy total energy (eV)
    atomic_energies per-atom energies (eV)
    virial total virial, (3, 3) (eV)
    atomic_virials per-atom virials, (N, 3, 3) (eV)
    stress stress, (3, 3) (GPa), positive under tension: −virial / volume
    elastic constants (6, 6) (GPa) in Voigt order xx, yy, zz, yz, xz, xy
    force_constants second derivatives of the energy with respect to the positions of two atoms, (n, nS, 3, 3) (eV/Å²), between the atoms of a cell and of its supercell
    ForceConstants the second order force constants second of a cell in a supercell, from an array or a file; phonons() gives their Phonons
    second of ForceConstants: the second order force constants, (n, nS, 3, 3) (eV/Å²), as the force_constants of Phonons
    third of ForceConstants: the third order force constants, (n, nS, nS, 3, 3, 3) (eV/Å³), third derivatives of the energy with respect to the positions of an atom of the cell and two atoms of the supercell
    cutoff_third of force_constants: the longest distance in Å between any two of three atoms whose third order force constants are fitted
    free force constants the components of the force constants that the symmetry of the crystal, the exchange of the two atoms and the sum rule leave; parameters of a fit by force_constants
    displaced supercells supercells of a crystal with atoms moved from their sites, whose forces give the force constants: one atom along an axis ("finite") or every atom along a random direction, by plus and minus ("random")
    bond stiffness springs along (longitudinal) and across (transverse) the bonds of crystals, of stiffness a polynomial of the length of the bond for each pair of elements in each shell of neighbors, from bond_stiffness; its force_constants give the phonons of any arrangement of the elements
    shell of bond_stiffness: the bonds no longer than its outer radius and longer than the radius before it, each with springs of their own
    FORCE_CONSTANTS phonopy's text file of force constants (eV/Å²), compact (the atoms of the cell) or full (every atom of the supercell); ForceConstants.write, read_force_constants
    frequencies of vibrations, in THz; imaginary ones as negative numbers
    wave vector q in units of the reciprocal lattice vectors of the cell: (h, k, l) is h b1 + k b2 + l b3, with ai · bj = 2π δij
    mesh number of wave vectors along each reciprocal lattice vector, of a mesh through Γ
    primitive cell the smallest periodic cell of a crystal, from primitive_cell
    space group the operations that map a crystal onto itself, from SpaceGroup: number (1 to 230), symbol (Hermann–Mauguin), rotations and translations (Cartesian, Å)
    equivalent for each atom, the first atom that symmetry makes it equivalent to
    symmetry whether phonons and elastic constants use the symmetry of the crystal, found by spglib: True, False, or None, whenever spglib is installed
    gibbs_energy Gibbs energy per atom (eV), with the static energy, the free energy of the vibrations and the pressure times the volume
    free_energy Helmholtz free energy (eV); of the vibrations in ThermalProperties, with the zero-point energy
    mode Grüneisen parameter −(V/ω) dω/dV of a mode, from Phonons.gruneisen
    thermal displacements ⟨uαuβ⟩ (Å²) of each atom of a crystal by its vibrations, with the zero point motion, (T, n, 3, 3), from Phonons.thermal_displacements
    heat flux the energy carried through a unit area per unit time by the atoms, (eV Å/ps) times the volume as LAMMPS's compute heat/flux gives it; the Green–Kubo conductivity integrates its correlation
    kappa thermal conductivity (W/(m K)), (T, 3, 3), of the phonons in the relaxation-time approximation, from thermal_conductivity
    gamma of ThermalConductivity: the imaginary part of the self energy of each mode by three-phonon scattering (THz), half its linewidth
    lifetimes of phonons (ps), 1/(4π gamma)
    sigma the width of a Gaussian in the units of what it spreads, THz for frequencies
    thermal_expansion volumetric thermal expansion coefficient (1/K)
    bulk_modulus isothermal bulk modulus (GPa)
    young_modulus stress over strain of a stretch along a direction with no stress across it (GPa); without a direction, that of a polycrystal
    poisson_ratio contraction along a transverse direction over the stretch along another; without them, that of a polycrystal
    linear_compressibility relative shortening along a direction per unit of hydrostatic pressure (1/GPa)
    universal_anisotropy the universal anisotropy index of elastic constants: zero for an isotropic crystal, positive otherwise
    stress fluctuations elastic constants from one run at constant volume: the mean Born term, minus V/kT times the covariance of the virial stress, plus the kinetic term; md_elastic_constants(..., method="fluctuation")
    direction, normal, transverse of elastic constants: a vector (3,) or vectors (N, 3) in the axes of the box, of any length; the direction of a stretch or shear, the normal of the plane sheared, the direction of a contraction across a stretch
    extreme "min" or "max": the smallest or largest value of a quantity of two directions over the second
    isothermal, adiabatic elastic constants at constant temperature, and at constant entropy (those of sound waves)
    errors standard errors of the means of a molecular dynamics result, from block averages, in its units
    thermo the thermodynamic state of a molecular dynamics run, a table of step, time, temperature, potential_energy, kinetic_energy, total_energy, pressure, volume and stress
    ensemble "nve", "nvt" or "npt"
    heat_capacity heat capacity per atom (eV/K): at constant volume of the vibrations, at constant pressure in the quasi-harmonic approximation
    descriptors (N, dim) per-atom inputs of the network of a machine-learned potential, such as those of NEP.descriptors

    list of elements elements element_list
    fractions of elements fractions element_ratio

    Burgers vector (Å), dislocation line and normal of the cut burgers, line, normal b, xi, n

    distance between the Shockley partials of a dislocation (Å) dissociation partial_distance
    thickness of the fixed outer shell of a dislocation cylinder (Å) fixed fix_width
    shells of neighbors of a lattice shells nshell

    seeds of grains, or their number grains seed_number, seed_position
    prebuilt neighbor list neighbors= (keyword-only)
    systems of a trajectory frames pos_list, traj
    average over every time origin window (bool) mode
    smallest Voronoi face (Å²), fraction of the cell's surface, shortest edge (Å) face_threshold, relative_face_threshold, edge_threshold area_threshold
    neighbors that share a Voronoi face voronoi=True use_voronoi

    Names of functions and methods¶

    • Analyses are classes named after what they compute, such as CommonNeighborAnalysis. Parameters are given to the constructor and validated there; compute(system, ...) returns a result object.
    • Changing a System uses with_* methods and select, which return a new System; a System itself has no other methods. Geometric transformations such as replicate and wrap are functions in mdapy.transform.
    • Building structures uses build_* functions.
    • Reading and writing uses read, write and read_trajectory.
    • Conversion to other packages lives in mdapy.interop, as to_* and from_* functions.
    • get_* only reads a value; it never starts a computation.

    Units and sign conventions are listed in Units and conventions.
