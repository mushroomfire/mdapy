    Plane-wave DFT¶

    Under development

    MDAPY's density functional theory is new. It takes norm-conserving, ultrasoft and PAW pseudopotentials, runs on one NVIDIA GPU (or AMD GPU, built from source) per configuration, and its speed is still being worked on. Its results are those of Quantum ESPRESSO on the same input, as below.

    from pathlib import Path

from mdapy.calculators import DFT
from mdapy.io import read, write

dft = DFT(
    600.0,  # eV, the cutoff of the plane waves
    pseudopotentials="pseudo-dojo/nc-sr-04_pbe_standard",  # Al.upf, Ni.upf, ...
    kspacing=0.2,  # 1/Å
    smearing="marzari-vanderbilt",
    sigma=0.1,  # eV
    magmoms={"Ni": 0.6},
)
frames = []
for path in sorted(Path("structures").glob("*.xyz")):
    system = read(path)
    frames.append(dft.compute(system, ("energy", "forces", "virial")).attach(system))
write("train.xyz", frames)  # the energy, virial, forces and parameters of each frame

    DFT(encut, *, ecutrho=None, pseudopotentials=None, kpoints=None, kspacing=0.25, smearing="gaussian", sigma=0.1, magmoms=None, hubbard=None, functional=None, tolerance=1e-6, max_iterations=100, mixing=None, magnetic_mixing=1.0, kerker=1.5, preconditioner="kerker", bands=None, seed=0, gpu=None, restart=False, symmetry=None, precision="double", kernels="mdapy", comm=None, log=None, start=None) computes the energies, forces and virials of periodic cells by Kohn–Sham density functional theory in plane waves, on an NVIDIA GPU, as a calculator of MDAPY. It suits the labels of training sets: configurations of up to a few hundred atoms, one at a time. It runs in the GPU part of MDAPY, the package mdapy-cuda12 for GPUs from the V100 on, on MDAPY's own kernels:

    pip install "mdapy[cuda12]"

    To compare them with NVIDIA's cuBLAS, cuFFT and cuSOLVER (kernels="vendor"), the extra dft installs those from NVIDIA's packages, pip install "mdapy[cuda12,dft]" (see NVIDIA's libraries for the DFT).

    For an AMD GPU the same sources are compiled with HIP of ROCm 6 into the package mdapy-rocm6, in a clone of MDAPY:

    pip install ./rocm6 -Ccmake.define.CMAKE_HIP_ARCHITECTURES=gfx90a

    gfx90a names the MI200 series. The build needs HIP, hipBLAS, hipFFT and hipSOLVER of ROCm, the run the HIP runtime, as module load rocm gives it on a cluster, and kernels="vendor" the libraries of ROCm too, which it loads from the folders of LD_LIBRARY_PATH, $ROCM_PATH/lib or /opt/rocm/lib. There the calculations of several threads take turns on the GPU instead of running at once. Without the GPU part, or the libraries of the vendor for kernels="vendor", DFT raises an error that says what to install.

    Name Type Default Unit Meaning
    encut float64 eV the kinetic energy cutoff of the plane waves, as VASP's ENCUT
    ecutrho float64 4 encut, 8 with ultrasoft or PAW pseudopotentials eV the cutoff of the density, as QE's ecutrho
    pseudopotentials mapping or path $MDAPY_PSEUDO_DIR UPF files by element, or a folder with {element}.upf or one {element}_*.upf of each element
    kpoints (3,) int32 None a Gamma-centered mesh of k-points
    kspacing float64 0.25 1/Å without kpoints, the largest spacing of the mesh, 2π included, as VASP's KSPACING
    smearing str "gaussian" "gaussian", "fermi-dirac", "methfessel-paxton" (first order), "marzari-vanderbilt", or "fixed" for insulators
    sigma float64 0.1 eV the width of the smearing
    magmoms mapping or (N,) float64 None Bohr magneton the first magnetic moments, by element or one per atom; with them the spins are collinear
    hubbard mapping of str to (str, float64) None eV DFT+U: for an element the label of one of its pseudo-orbitals, such as "3d", and U
    functional str that of the pseudopotentials, as Quantum ESPRESSO takes it "pbe", "pbe_pw", "pbesol", "pbesol_pw", "lda_pw" or "lda_pw_mod"
    tolerance float64 1e-6 eV the self-consistent field stops when the energy changes by less and the Hartree energy of the change of the density is below it
    max_iterations int32 100 the steps of the self-consistent field before it fails
    mixing float64 None the share of the residual of the density taken at each step; None for 0.7 without spin, as Quantum ESPRESSO, and 0.4 with it
    magnetic_mixing float64 1.0 with spin, the share of the residual of the magnetization taken at each step, as VASP's AMIX_MAG
    kerker float64 1.5 1/Å the wave vector of Kerker's preconditioner of the mixing, 0 for none
    preconditioner str "kerker" "kerker", "ldos" of the mixing of the total density: "ldos" from the local density of states at the Fermi level of each step (Herbst and Levitt 2021), which screens the metal and not the vacuum or the insulator of a cell: slabs, surfaces, and magnetic alloys whose moments Kerker's lets wander or fall
    bands int32 None bands per k-point and spin; by default four or 10 % more than the occupied ones for fixed occupations, else the larger of 1.2 N/2 and N/2 + 4 for N electrons
    symmetry bool None the symmetry of the crystal, found by spglib: the irreducible k-points alone, the density, the occupations of the projectors, the forces and the stress made symmetric; by default when spglib is installed
    seed int32 0 seed of the random first bands
    gpu int32 None the GPU of this process, by its index among those it sees; by default the first
    restart bool or str False True, "atomic", "second_order" start each computation from the bands and the density of the last, as the steps of a relaxation or of molecular dynamics may; "second_order" extrapolates the density from the last three computations (Arias et al. 1992), fewer iterations along molecular dynamics; labels of a training set should start cold
    moment_radius float64 None Å with spin, the radius of the spheres within which the magnetic moment of each atom is integrated; by default half the shortest distance between two atoms of the cell, their periodic images included, the same for all atoms, so that the spheres do not overlap
    precision str "double" "double", "mixed", "auto" "mixed": the first steps of the self-consistent field in single precision, the last in double, for GPUs of little double precision (most cards for games and workstations); the label is that of double precision, last["single_steps"] the steps in single precision. "auto": mixed on those GPUs, double on V100, A100, H100 and GH200. Mixed precision holds about a tenth more of the GPU's memory at its peak: a cell that only just fits in double runs with "double"
    kernels str "mdapy" "mdapy", "vendor" Whose kernels run on the GPU. "mdapy": those of MDAPY, the fast Fourier transforms, the dense linear algebra (the products of matrices and the triangular solves), the Hermitian eigensolver and the Cholesky factorization, which is all the DFT takes from the libraries of the vendor, which it does not load; only sizes of a grid given that are not products of 2, 3, 5 and 7 go to the FFT of the vendor. "vendor": the GPU libraries of the vendor throughout (cuFFT, cuBLAS and cuSOLVER on NVIDIA GPUs, of the extra dft; hipFFT, hipBLAS and hipSOLVER on AMD GPUs). The results agree to rounding; last["provenance"] keeps which one ran
    comm mpi4py communicator None processes that compute each configuration together, one GPU each, sharing its k-points and spins; every process calls compute with the same system and gets the whole result; without gpu each takes the GPU of its rank on its machine
    log path or stream None where every step of the self-consistent field of each computation is written, with the parameters it took (below)
    start path None a ground state that save wrote, which the first computation starts from (below)
    Attribute or method Meaning
    cutoff 0: the calculator needs no neighbor list
    compute(system, properties, *, neighbors=None) the free energy in eV, the forces in eV/Å and, when asked, the virial in eV; neighbors is not used
    last of the last computation: iterations, restarted, fermi (eV), magnetization and absolute_magnetization (Bohr magnetons, with spin), magnetic_moments ((N,) float64 Bohr magnetons, with spin: the magnetization density, spin up minus spin down, integrated within a sphere of moment_radius around each atom, in the order of the atoms) and moment_radius (Å, the radius used), net_force (eV/Å), kpoints, grid, gpu_memory (the memory of the GPU in MiB), provenance, calculation (its number among the computations of the calculator) and parameters (every parameter it took, below)
    log, start the log and the ground state given
    save(path) writes the ground state of the last computation (below)

    The system must be periodic along a, b and c and have an element column. The energy is the free energy of the smearing, VASP's TOTEN. provenance holds what makes the labels of a set comparable: the SHA-256 of each pseudopotential, the cutoff, the mesh, the smearing and its width, the functional, the spins, the grid and the bands. Labels of a set should share them. It also holds the kernels, which changes the results by rounding alone. With MDAPY's kernels the same computation on the same GPU gives the same numbers to the last bit at each run; the libraries of the vendor do not promise it when the k-points are solved side by side.

    The magnetic moments are those of the smooth density on the grid, with the augmentation charges of ultrasoft and PAW pseudopotentials but not the one-center corrections of PAW, within spheres that do not overlap: they depend on the radius, and their sum is less than magnetization, which counts the whole cell. Compare them across labels of a set only with the same moment_radius.

    The log and the parameters of each computation¶

    from mdapy.calculators import DFT
from mdapy.io import read, write

dft = DFT(300.0, kpoints=(2, 2, 2), sigma=0.2, log="dft.log")
system = read("al4.xyz")
write("labels.xyz", dft.compute(system, ("energy", "forces", "virial")).attach(system))

    log takes a path, or a stream such as sys.stdout. The first computation of a calculator writes the file anew, and the next ones add to it. The heading names MDAPY, the GPU and the machine. Each computation then writes:

    • a line with its atoms, the mesh and its irreducible k-points, the operations of the symmetry, the bands and the FFT grid;
    • its parameters as one line of JSON, when they differ from the last ones written;
    • a row for every step of the self-consistent field, the steps in single precision of precision="mixed" marked where they begin and end;
    • its end: the steps, the seconds, the energy, the largest force and, with the virial, the pressure; or, if it stopped, where and why.

    Lines that are not rows start with #, so that numpy.loadtxt("dft.log") reads the rows of every computation as one table; its first column tells them apart.

    Column Unit Meaning
    calculation the number of the computation among those of the calculator, last["calculation"]
    step the step of the self-consistent field, from 1
    free_energy eV the free energy of the step, of its input density (Harris–Foulkes); that of the last step is the label
    change eV from the step before, nan for the first
    residual eV the Hartree energy of the change of the density, which tolerance bounds
    magnetization Bohr magneton the moment of the cell, 0 without spin
    fermi eV the Fermi level
    davidson the iterations of the eigensolver, summed over the k-points and spins
    wall s the seconds since the self-consistent field began

    The log costs the run nothing it does not already have. The numbers of a step are those the self-consistent field tests its convergence with, already on the processor, so nothing more comes from the GPU. Without a log, no step is reported. Under MPI the first process writes; threads that share a GPU give each calculator its own file.

    What each label keeps¶

    Each computation keeps every parameter it took in last["parameters"], its defaults and "auto" resolved: the mixing, ecutrho, the mesh, the bands, the symmetry, the precision and the kernels, as they ran. Its result keeps them in info["dft"], which attach puts into the frame and write into each frame of an extended XYZ file. A label so says how it was computed, with or without a log. A later version of MDAPY, whose defaults may change, computes it again from them:

    from mdapy.calculators import DFT
from mdapy.io import read

frame = read("labels.xyz")
record = frame.info["dft"]
again = DFT(pseudopotentials="pseudo-dojo", **record["arguments"])
print(again.compute(frame).energy, frame.info["energy"])
    Key Meaning
    mdapy the version of MDAPY
    arguments the arguments of DFT that give the same computation: those of the table above but pseudopotentials, gpu, comm, log and start
    pseudopotentials of each element, the name of its file and its SHA-256: another file of the same name is another pseudopotential
    internal the settings of the self-consistent field that DFT does not take, such as the 8 steps of the history of Pulay's mixing, by their names inside, in eV and Å as the rest
    environment the variables MDAPY_DFT_* that were set
    gpu, processes the GPU, and the processes of comm

    The record gives the same computation, not the same digits: another GPU, or other kernels, add in another order, and the results agree to the tolerance.

    The ground state in a file¶

    save writes the ground state of the last computation, and start takes it up again: the first computation then starts from its density, and from the occupations of the projectors of PAW or DFT+U, in place of those of the free atoms, as to compute the same structure with another tolerance or more steps of the self-consistent field. Its first step solves the bands tightly in that potential, and it converges in a few steps: 4 atoms of aluminium in 3 instead of 7, PAW silicon in 4 instead of 10.

    from mdapy.calculators import DFT
from mdapy.io import read

system = read("al32.xyz")
dft = DFT(500.0, kspacing=0.2)
dft.compute(system)
dft.save("al32.npz")

tighter = DFT(500.0, kspacing=0.2, tolerance=1e-9, start="al32.npz")
result = tighter.compute(system)  # in a few steps

    The file is NumPy's .npz, which numpy.load(path) reads without pickles. The file is written whole or not at all, by the first process under MPI. It holds:

    Array Shape Unit Meaning
    format, version "MDAPY DFT ground state" and 1, the version of the file
    parameters the record of the computation, as last["parameters"], as JSON
    elements (N,) str the atoms
    cell, positions (3, 3), (N, 3) float64 Å the rows of the cell, and the atoms as given
    energy, fermi float64 eV the free energy and the Fermi level
    forces (N, 3) float64 eV/Å the forces
    density (spins, n1, n2, n3) float64 electrons/Å³ on the FFT grid of the density, with the augmentation charges; with spin, up then down
    kpoints, weights (K, 3), (K,) float64 the irreducible k-points in fractions of the reciprocal vectors, and their weights, adding to 1
    eigenvalues (spins, K, bands) float64 eV the bands
    occupations (spins, K, bands) float64 of each band, from 0 to 1 (Methfessel–Paxton and Marzari–Vanderbilt a little beyond); a band holds 2 electrons without spin, 1 with
    becsum (spins, pairs) float64 with PAW or DFT+U, the occupations of the projectors
    magnetic_moments (N,) float64 Bohr magneton with spin, as last["magnetic_moments"]

    spins is 1 without spin and 2 with. The wave functions are not kept: the density takes a computation back to its ground state in a few steps.

    A computation started from a file must be of its elements, in the same order, its cell and atoms to 1e-6 Å (an atom moved by a vector of the cell is the same), and its pseudopotentials by their SHA-256; and, as the density is on its grid, of its cutoffs, symmetry and spin. InvalidParameter says which differs. save before a computation raises InvalidParameter, as does a file that is not a ground state or one of a later version.

    In molecular dynamics¶

    The DFT's log and that of the dynamics go to two files:

    from mdapy.calculators import DFT
from mdapy.io import read
from mdapy.simulation import MolecularDynamics

dft = DFT(500.0, kspacing=0.3, restart="second_order", log="dft.log")
with MolecularDynamics(read("al32.xyz"), dft, timestep=0.002, log="md.log") as md:
    md.velocities(300, seed=1)
    md.run(1000, ensemble="nvt", temperature=300, thermo=10)

    md.log holds a row every 10 steps of the dynamics; dft.log holds every step of the self-consistent field of each of them. The same file for both raises InvalidParameter. A thousand steps of about ten steps of the self-consistent field each make some 10 000 rows, about 1.5 MB.

    Many GPUs¶

    A configuration runs on one GPU. Many configurations, as the labels of a training set, share the GPUs of a machine as processes of their own, one GPU each, started by mpirun:

    from mpi4py import MPI

from mdapy.calculators import DFT
from mdapy.io import read, write

rank, size = MPI.COMM_WORLD.Get_rank(), MPI.COMM_WORLD.Get_size()
dft = DFT(600.0, kspacing=0.2, gpu=rank % 4)  # 4 GPUs on each machine
mine = [read(f"structures/{i}.xyz") for i in range(rank, 1000, size)]
labelled = [dft.compute(s, ("energy", "forces", "virial")).attach(s) for s in mine]
write(f"train-{rank}.xyz", labelled)

    A small cell leaves most of a GPU idle. Its k-points are solved side by side on threads of the processor by themselves, and several configurations share one GPU from threads of one process, each with a calculator of its own:

    from concurrent.futures import ThreadPoolExecutor

from mdapy.calculators import DFT
from mdapy.io import read_trajectory, write

structures = list(read_trajectory("structures.xyz"))


def label(part):
    dft = DFT(600.0, kspacing=0.2)
    return [dft.compute(s, ("energy", "forces", "virial")).attach(s) for s in part]


with ThreadPoolExecutor(4) as pool:
    parts = list(pool.map(label, [structures[i::4] for i in range(4)]))
write("train.xyz", [s for part in parts for s in part])

    Four to eight threads fill a GPU with cells of a few dozen atoms; a cell of hundreds of atoms fills it alone. Configurations side by side hold the sum of their memory.

    One configuration of many k-points or of two spins can also run on several GPUs: with comm, the processes share its pairs of spin and k-point, each GPU holding the bands of its own, and every process gets the whole result. This pays when a configuration has at least as many such pairs as GPUs.

    from mpi4py import MPI

from mdapy.calculators import DFT
from mdapy.io import read

dft = DFT(600.0, kpoints=(4, 4, 4), magmoms={"Fe": 2.5}, comm=MPI.COMM_WORLD)
result = dft.compute(read("fe54.xyz"), ("energy", "forces", "virial"))
if MPI.COMM_WORLD.Get_rank() == 0:
    print(result.energy)

    Memory of the GPU¶

    A configuration holds on the GPU the bands of each of its k-points and spins, n plane waves by the bands in complex numbers of 16 bytes, with n about \(\Omega\,(2E)^{3/2}/6\pi^2\) for a cell of Ω bohr³ and encut E in hartree (half of that at Γ alone, where the bands are real); they take most of its memory when it has many k-points. Besides them it holds the projectors of the atoms, the basis of the eigensolver (twice the bands), the boxes of the Fourier transforms (256 MiB at most) and the densities and potentials on the grid. The CUDA context takes 0.3 to 0.6 GiB more, and the libraries of the vendor (kernels="vendor") about 0.4 GiB for each host thread that solves k-points on an H100 or GH200 (0.05 GiB on a V100): small cells of many k-points run eight. Measured on a GH200 with the libraries of the vendor in double precision, for cells perturbed as those of a training set, so that time reversal alone reduces their k-points:

    Cell Cutoff, kspacing k-points × spins Bands In use (GiB) On the GPU (GiB)
    Al4, fcc 600 eV, 0.15 1/Å 666 × 1 10 0.4 4.4
    Al32 600 eV, 0.15 1/Å 112 × 1 58 2.4 3.5
    Al108 600 eV, Γ 1 × 1 195 2.6 3.6
    Al108 600 eV, 0.15 1/Å 36 × 1 195 10.3 11.4
    Fe16, bcc, 3 μB 900 eV, 0.2 1/Å 112 × 2 154 6.4 7.8
    Fe54, bcc, 3 μB 900 eV, 0.2 1/Å 36 × 2 519 25.0 26.8

    "In use" is the peak of the arrays of the DFT, "on the GPU" that of the device, with the context and the libraries. Mixed precision holds 5 to 10 % more. Configurations side by side hold the sum of theirs: eight Al32 at once hold 19.2 GiB and fit a V100 of 32 GB, sixteen do not. A run keeps what it uses in a memory pool and gives back to the device, at its end, what the pool holds beyond the use of its steps: a process holds the memory of its last runs, not the most it ever took. last["gpu_memory"] holds the largest at the end of the steps, in MiB: in_use by the arrays of the DFT and reserved by its pool, of the whole process, the configurations of its threads together, and device in use on the GPU, by the CUDA context, the libraries and other processes too. See the measurements.

    What it computes¶

    • Plane waves of kinetic energy up to encut at each k-point of the mesh; k-points related by time reversal count once. The bands live on the FFT grid of four times the cutoff, the density and the potentials on that of ecutrho, chosen as Quantum ESPRESSO chooses them: along each vector at least 2 m + 1 points, m the largest Miller index of the sphere, with 2, 3 and 5 as the only factors, and with the symmetry a multiple of the denominators of its fractional translations (4 in diamond). When the mesh is Γ alone, as for large cells, the bands are real: they keep half of the plane waves, the other half being their complex conjugates, with real Fourier transforms and real products of the bands, for the same results in less time and half the memory.
    • Pseudopotentials: in UPF 2, norm-conserving, ultrasoft or PAW, with their projectors, the core charge of the nonlinear core correction and, ultrasoft and PAW, their augmentation charges by angular momentum (q_with_l, as the files of a recent ld1.x or of atompaw); and in UPF 1, norm-conserving or ultrasoft, such as GBRV's, whose augmentation charges are pseudized inside rinner as Quantum ESPRESSO pseudizes them. Every file of SSSP 1.3 is read, and its folder can be given as pseudopotentials. The tested ones are PseudoDojo's norm-conserving tables, the ultrasoft and PAW files of pslibrary 1.0.0, GBRV's ultrasoft tungsten and atompaw's lanthanum.
    • Ultrasoft pseudopotentials solve H ψ = ε S ψ with the overlap S = 1 + Σ |β⟩ q ⟨β|; the strengths of the projectors of each atom follow the potential at every step, and the density adds the augmentation charges.
    • PAW adds to that the one-center terms of each atom, on its radial mesh: the all-electron and pseudo densities of the occupations of its projectors, their Hartree and exchange-correlation energies, and the strengths these add to the projectors. The occupations are mixed along with the density, as Quantum ESPRESSO mixes them. The energy is Quantum ESPRESSO's total energy, without the energy of the frozen core.
    • Exchange and correlation: LDA (Perdew–Wang), PBE and PBEsol, with and without spin. "pbe_pw" is PBE on Perdew and Wang's correlation with the constants of their paper, as Quantum ESPRESSO's PBE, and the default for PBE pseudopotentials. "pbe" uses those of libxc; the two differ by about 1e-6 of the correlation energy, 0.01 to 0.25 meV per atom. "pbesol_pw" and "pbesol" are PBEsol likewise; a PBEsol run may take PBE pseudopotentials, as Quantum ESPRESSO's input_dft does, but PBEsol ones are better.
    • DFT+U of Dudarev et al. (1998): E_U = Σ (U/2) Tr[n(1 − n)] over the Hubbard atoms and spins, n the occupations of the pseudo-orbital named in hubbard on Quantum ESPRESSO's atomic projectors, the pseudo-orbitals as they are, neither orthogonalized nor normalized (Cococcioni and de Gironcoli 2005). The occupations start as Quantum ESPRESSO starts them, the majority spin filled first, and are mixed with the density; the forces and the stress include them. U belongs to its projectors: values fitted with those of other codes, such as the spheres of VASP's PAW or the orthogonalized orbitals of Quantum ESPRESSO's linear response, do not carry over. Norm-conserving pseudopotentials only, for now.
    • The self-consistent field: the bands by block Davidson, from the pseudo-orbitals of the free atoms at the start, the density mixed by Pulay's method in the metric of Kresse and Furthmüller with Kerker's preconditioner. A run that does not converge raises. With restart, a run starts from the bands of the last, plane wave by plane wave, and from its density with the free atoms moved from where they were to where they are (restart=True, Quantum ESPRESSO's pot_extrapolation='atomic'); with restart="second_order" the density less its free atoms is extrapolated from the last three runs, d(t+1) = d(t) + α (d(t) − d(t−1)) + β (d(t−1) − d(t−2)), α and β fitted to the displacements of the atoms (one step: the atomic; two: α = 1, β = 0). A run of other k-points, bands, spins or a number of atoms starts cold.
    • Symmetry: the operations of the crystal that keep its atoms, their kinds and first moments, found by spglib to 1e-5 bohr. The k-points of the mesh reduce to the irreducible ones, k equivalent to R^T k and to −k. The output density is the average of its images on the sphere of reciprocal space, so the fractional translations need not fit the FFT grid; the occupations of the projectors and the one-center strengths of PAW are averaged with the rotations of the real harmonics, and the forces and the stress over the operations. A configuration without symmetry, as most of a training set, runs on the mesh with time reversal alone.
    • Forces with Quantum ESPRESSO's correction for a density not quite self-consistent. Their mean, which only the FFT grid leaves, is taken out and kept in last["net_force"].
    • The virial is the derivative of the energy under a strain that keeps the Miller indices of the plane waves. It lacks the Pulay stress of an incomplete basis: test the cutoff, as with VASP, by the change of the stress.

    The constructor raises InvalidParameter for a cutoff, width, tolerance or mesh that is not positive, an unknown smearing, or a folder that does not exist. compute raises InvalidParameter for a box that is not periodic along a, b and c, an element without a pseudopotential, or a moment for each atom of the wrong number; ValueError for a Hubbard orbital that is not in the file or a Hubbard element with an ultrasoft or PAW pseudopotential; MissingProperty without an element column; and CalculationFailed when the self-consistent field does not converge, with its last energies.

    When the self-consistent field does not converge¶

    The defaults suit bulk cells. Cells of metal and vacuum, surfaces, vacancies, and magnetic alloys converge better with the preconditioner of the local density of states, which screens the metal and not the vacuum, and keeps the moments of a magnet from wandering among nearby orders or falling to none:

    from mdapy.calculators import DFT
from mdapy.errors import CalculationFailed

dft = DFT(600.0, magmoms={"Cr": -2.0, "Co": 3.0, "Ni": 1.2}, preconditioner="ldos")
try:
    result = dft.compute(cell, ("energy", "forces", "virial"))
except CalculationFailed:  # slower still: a smaller share of the residual, more steps
    dft = DFT(600.0, magmoms={"Cr": -2.0, "Co": 3.0, "Ni": 1.2}, preconditioner="ldos",
              mixing=0.2, max_iterations=200)  # fmt: skip
    result = dft.compute(cell, ("energy", "forces", "virial"))
print(dft.last["iterations"], dft.last["magnetization"])

    A magnet can converge to more than one state, and the first moments choose among them. Cells of four atoms of Co2CrNi, started with −1, 1.5 and 0.6 Bohr magnetons on Cr, Co and Ni, ended in 6 of 20 cells in states of smaller moments, or none, 50 to 110 meV per atom above a ferrimagnetic one; started with twice those moments, as above, all reached it, in fewer steps. In these cells it made no difference whether the first moment sat over the valence or in the d shell; its size did. Start each atom at or above the moment it is expected to carry, its high-spin moment when unsure. A state of small moments, once converged, holds: restarted with its magnetization doubled, it came back. Compare last["magnetization"] and last["absolute_magnetization"] across the labels of a set, and start them alike.

    How the results compare with Quantum ESPRESSO¶

    Its speed against Quantum ESPRESSO on GPUs and processors is on its own page. The verification adds the equations of state of 72 elements against all-electron codes.

    Quantum ESPRESSO 7.6 (pw.x) runs on the same cells, PseudoDojo files, cutoff, k-points, FFT grid and smearing, with pbe_pw. The tests run it each time. The differences:

    Silicon, diamond, aluminium (four smearings) bcc iron, fcc nickel, antiferromagnetic chromium, with spin
    total energy and each of its terms below 1e-7 hartree per atom below 1e-7 hartree per atom once the rounded constant below is allowed for
    eigenvalues of the occupied bands, Fermi level below 1e-7 hartree below 1e-6 hartree
    forces below 1e-7 hartree/bohr
    stress, in sheared cells 0.003 kbar 0.02 kbar
    magnetization below 1e-4 Bohr magneton

    With the ultrasoft files of pslibrary 1.0.0, on one grid and on two (ecutrho 4 and 8 times encut), silicon and aluminium agree in every term of the energy, the occupied eigenvalues and the Fermi level to 1e-7 hartree and in the forces to 1e-7 hartree/bohr; bcc iron with spin in the energy to 1e-7 hartree per atom once the rounded constant is allowed for, and in the magnetization to 1e-6 Bohr magneton. The stress of sheared silicon agrees to 0.01 kbar, that of sheared iron with spin to 0.03 kbar, and both with differences of strained cells at a fixed basis to 1e-7 hartree.

    With the PAW files of pslibrary 1.0.0 (ecutrho 8 times encut), aluminium, silicon of two displaced atoms and bcc iron with spin agree in the energy to 1e-8 hartree per atom (iron once the rounded constant is allowed for), in the occupied eigenvalues to 1e-7 hartree, in the forces to 1e-7 hartree/bohr, in the stress to 0.01 kbar and, iron, in the magnetization to 1e-6 Bohr magneton.

    With DFT+U against Quantum ESPRESSO's HUBBARD (atomic), antiferromagnetic NiO of four displaced atoms with U = 6 eV on the 3d of nickel, and two displaced atoms of copper without spin with U = 4 eV, agree in the energy to 1e-8 hartree per atom (NiO once the rounded constant is allowed for), in the Hubbard energy to the 8 digits Quantum ESPRESSO prints, in the traces of the occupations to 1e-5, in the forces to 1e-6 hartree/bohr, in the stress to 0.003 kbar, in the occupied eigenvalues to 1e-6 hartree and, NiO, in the magnetization to 1e-6 Bohr magneton.

    What differs is Quantum ESPRESSO's own:

    • Its Ewald sum is converged to about 1e-7 Ry.
    • Its spin-polarized PBE correlation takes γ = 0.031091, rounded, where its paramagnetic one takes (1 − ln 2)/π². This moves the energy of iron by 1e-6 hartree, and with PAW by 4e-6 more through the one-center densities. A NumPy evaluation of the difference on MDAPY's densities accounts for it to 1e-8.

    The forces also agree with differences of the energy of displaced atoms, to 1e-7 hartree/bohr. The stress agrees with differences of strained cells whose plane waves keep their Miller indices, to 5e-8 hartree.
